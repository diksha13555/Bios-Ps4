Every motion has proof.

Most systems rebuild a 3D hand for every frame and stop there. MotionTruth adds the evidence: how trustworthy each frame is, where tracking broke, and what needs a human before a robot learns from it.

What we claim, and what we don’t. Confidence does not prove the 3D coordinates are correct, and estimated world landmarks do not establish true global position. MotionTruth is a motion-quality estimate built from confidence, landmark visibility, tracking gaps, frame-to-frame jumps and smoothness, and it should be validated against manually inspected clips.
